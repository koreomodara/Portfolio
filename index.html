About

A little about me

I design and build digital products that solve problems people stopped expecting anyone to fix — reducing administrative work by 60% for behavioral health organizations, doubling mobile notification opt‑ins, and redesigning the things everyone uses but nobody questions.

I'm Kore — a product designer with a B.A. in Media Arts and Sciences from Arizona State University. I'm drawn to the friction most designers skip past: the fifteenth screen nobody wants to build, the button that shouldn't exist, the flow that loses people every single time.

I live in Figma, occasionally in the browser, and constantly in the question of whether this actually needs to be here. I care about research that changes decisions, not research that fills a slide deck.

When I'm not designing I'm probably rearranging my desk for the third time, or making a case for why sticky notes are a legitimate design tool.

I'm cracked at GamePigeon Word Hunt. 8 Ball? Not so much…
I love #puzzling, currently working on a Lego minifigure puzzle.
I wrote an essay about the impact of iOS 7 in high school, ask me about it.
